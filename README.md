# Banking Customer Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer data from a banking dataset to identify customer demographics, financial patterns, loyalty levels, and risk distribution using Python, Jupyter Notebook, Excel, and Power BI.

The project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and business-oriented insights that can support data-driven decision-making in the banking sector.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics and financial characteristics.
- Perform data cleaning and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Segment customers based on income and age.
- Analyze relationships between income, deposits, savings, loans, and credit-related variables.
- Identify patterns in customer loyalty and risk levels.
- Present key findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 📊 Project Workflow

1. Loaded and explored the banking customer dataset.
2. Checked the dataset structure, data types, missing values, and duplicate Client IDs.
3. Performed descriptive statistical analysis.
4. Analyzed categorical and numerical variables.
5. Created new features:
   - Income Band
   - Age Band
   - Join Year
   - Join Month
   - Join Month Name
6. Performed correlation and relationship analysis.
7. Identified business-oriented insights from customer financial data.
8. Built a Power BI dashboard to visualize key customer and financial metrics.

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- Total Customers
- Average Estimated Income
- Total Bank Deposits
- Total Bank Loans
- Customers by Income Band
- Customers by Age Band
- Customer Loyalty Classification
- Customers by Risk Weighting

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

The analysis explores relationships between:

- Estimated Income and Checking Accounts
- Bank Deposits and Saving Accounts
- Bank Loans and Credit Card Balance
- Business Lending and Bank Loans
- Customer age and income segments
- Customer loyalty and risk distribution
